# Roadmap

Goal: turn Simple Dungeon into a combat-focused dungeon that takes advantage of the tools introduced with Trial Chambers (trial spawners, vaults, trial keys) and of what data packs can do in Minecraft 26.3.

Target version: **Minecraft 26.3** (data pack format 121).

## Done

### Update to Minecraft 26.3
- `pack.mcmeta` uses `min_format` / `max_format` (121).
- `data/dungeon/structures/` renamed to `data/dungeon/structure/` (required since 1.21).

### Broken jigsaw references
- Jigsaws pointing to the non-existent pool `minecraft:secondary_rooms` now point to `dungeon:secondary_rooms` (corridors, forge, kitchen, library, maze). Those exits never generated before.
- Jigsaws pointing to `dungeon:empty` now point to `minecraft:empty`.

### Step 1: Vanilla only (no mod dependencies)
The structures were built in a modded instance. Everything has been replaced with vanilla equivalents:
- **Kitchen**: Farmer's Delight and MrCrayfish's Furniture blocks replaced (barrels, smokers, cauldron, oak furniture). Modded food items replaced with vanilla food.
- **Forge**: Alex's Caves `mine_guardian` spawner now spawns blazes.
- **Maze, room 2, room 3**: Better Strongholds loot tables replaced with vanilla ones.
- **Red room**: Endless Biomes lamp replaced with a shroomlight, Alex's Mobs items in item frames replaced.
- **Undying room**: Alex's Mobs grapple replaced with wind charges.
- Forge-only entity attributes (`forge:entity_gravity`, `forge:step_height_addition`) removed.

### Kitchen loot tables
Kitchen barrels use dedicated loot tables instead of hand-filled inventories, in `data/dungeon/loot_table/chests/kitchen/`:

| Table | Content |
|---|---|
| `pantry` | Raw ingredients, poisonous potatoes, rare golden carrots |
| `meals` | Cooked food, stews, suspicious stew (mostly bad effects), rare golden apple |
| `desserts` | Cookies, pies, berries, melon, rare cake or golden apple |

About half of all rolls give rotten flesh: the kitchen has been abandoned for a long time.

The tables use the 26.3 loot table format: `modifier` with `type`, instead of `functions` with `function` used through 26.2.

### Anti-digging
Cracked deepslate bricks mark secret rooms that players open by mining them, so they are left alone.
- A player who breaks a deepslate brick, tile, polished or chiseled block, or a mud brick, inside the dungeon gets Mining Fatigue III for 30 s, with the elder guardian sound and an action bar message (at most once every 5 s). Detection uses `minecraft.mined` statistics and the `dungeon:in_dungeon` predicate. Cracked bricks are not tracked, and creative/spectator players are ignored.

### Maze rework: the well
`primary/maze_1` was rebuilt as a 25x18x25 room: three maze levels with 2-wide corridors around a 5x5 well. One way in (level 1, west) and one way out (level 3, east): players cross the three levels by finding the hidden ladders.
- A slime trial spawner (`dungeon:well/slime`, sizes 1 and 2) stands on a mud and soul sand pile at the bottom of the well. Normal waves eject consumables; ominous waves eject an ominous trial key or ominous consumables.
- An ominous vault hangs from a chain in the middle of the well, reachable from two level 3 balconies with no railing. Its loot (`dungeon:chests/well/vault_ominous`) is slime themed and better than a normal vault: emerald and iron blocks, Feather Falling diamond boots, diamond spear, slime blocks, enchanted golden apple, Mending, netherite upgrade template.
- Traps: a polished blackstone pressure plate right on top of an upward dispenser of lingering poison potions (2 on level 1, 2 on level 2, 1 on level 3).
- One secret room slot on level 2, with the usual breakable wall pattern (3 cracked bricks, cracked brick / jigsaw / cracked brick, 1 on top).
- Dead ends hold a decorated pot or candles. Candles are the only light: players bring their own.

The room is generated by the prototype script kept on the `prototype/maze-variants` branch (`prototype/maze/generate.py --export`).

### New room: the crypt
`primary/crypt_1` (23x14x25), added to `secondary_rooms`. Inspired by Skyrim's Nordic barrows: a nave facing north, dark deepslate with light tuff rims.
- Two tombs with trial spawners (`dungeon:crypt/skeleton`): skeletons; when ominous, skeletons and wither skeletons in chainmail with a quartz rib trim (`dungeon:equipment/crypt/*`). A trial spawner only knows normal or ominous: the Bad Omen level does not change the mobs.
- The jarl's tomb on a raised platform holds a vault that opens with the trial keys. Its loot (`dungeon:chests/crypt/vault`) favors weapons: enchanted spears, bows and crossbows, trimmed chainmail, the rib trim template and the skull banner pattern. It follows the vanilla reward structure (one rare or common item, 1 to 3 common items, 25% chance of a unique item) without Trial Chambers items.
- The vault is a normal vault, so the ominous spawner config ejects a normal trial key (`minecraft:spawners/trial_chamber/key`) instead of an ominous one.
- Burial alcoves with a decorated pot (archer or skull sherd) and a skeleton skull on top. Pots use `dungeon:pots/crypt/alcove`: mostly bones and bone meal, sometimes gold, an emerald or a diamond.

### Rooms no longer reach the surface
Corridors under the first room start about 28 blocks below the surface, but some pieces climbed and came out in the open air.
- `corridor/stairs_1` and `corridor/small_pit_1` only go down. Their lower jigsaws are named `dungeon:secondary_room_exit` (target and pool unchanged), so a parent can only attach to their upper jigsaw. A jigsaw attaches when its name matches the parent's target.
- Every room in `primary/` sends its exits to `dungeon:room_exits` instead of `secondary_rooms`: mostly stairs (30) and small pits (25), with a few flat corridors and no rooms. The dungeon goes tower, pit, `room_1`, then corridors and rooms, so the dungeon tends to go down after each room. Corridors keep using `secondary_rooms`.
- The well (`primary/maze_1`) still climbs 8 blocks (in on level 1, out on level 3): that is intended. The crypt climbs 3 blocks at most.
- `max_distance_from_center.vertical` does not help here: its box is centered on the tower, so it limits depth as much as height.

### Pacing: fewer corridors in a row
Play tests showed long chains of empty corridors and the same rooms coming back (kitchen, library). Causes: `size` was 7 and the fixed start (tower, pit, `room_1`) uses 3 levels of it, and `secondary_rooms` (the pool every corridor exit uses) was about half plain corridors. When a big room does not fit, the jigsaw tries the next element, so small rooms like the kitchen (15x20) win more often than the well (25x25) or the crypt (23x25).
- Rooms already exit through `dungeon:room_exits` (corridors only), so rooms and corridors alternate as soon as corridor exits lead mostly to rooms.
- `secondary_rooms` is now about 62% rooms, 26% corridors with content (the five below, `room_with_sand_1`, `room_jump`, `end_with_secret_1`) and 12% plain corridors. The big rooms weigh more (well and crypt 14, forge and library 10, rooms 2 and 3 9, kitchen 8) to make up for failing to fit more often.
- `size` goes from 7 to 9: one more room level per branch.

### Corridors with content
Five variants of `corridor/small_line_1`, with the same footprint (7x7x15) and jigsaws, in `secondary_rooms` and `room_exits` (where `small_line_1` drops from 10 to 4 so rooms still lead mostly down). Generated by `prototype/corridors/generate.py --export` on the `prototype/maze-variants` branch; the tripwire trap was designed by hand in game and copied from the test world.

| Piece | Content |
|---|---|
| `pots_1` | 4 urns on the benches and in the wide middle, candles, no mob |
| `collapsed_1` | Three rubble piles to walk around, the ceiling torn open on the rock (or cave) above, a half-buried pot |
| `spider_nest_1` | A trial spawner (`dungeon:corridor/spider`) sunk in the floor under cobwebs: 4 spiders (+1 per player); 6 with cave spiders when ominous; ejects consumables |
| `tripwire_1` | A tripwire just behind the pillars, hidden from the north, fires two arrow dispensers in the walls |
| `collapsed_nest_1` | `collapsed_1` with the spider spawner in the rubble |

The pots use `dungeon:pots/corridor`: arrows, bones, string, coal, nuggets, sometimes an emerald, an iron ingot or a diamond. The rubble is cobbled deepslate, so it can be mined without the anti-dig penalty; no cracked bricks are used.

## Next steps

### Pacing: small rooms and corridors with content
Trial Chambers feel full because there are many rooms and the corridors between them are short. The dungeon needs more small pieces between the big themed rooms.
- **Small combat rooms** (11x11 to 15x15, one trial spawner each): guard post (equipped zombies or vindicators), ossuary, spider nest, flooded room (drowned), cells (a prisoner villager to free), wind room (breezes, platforms), collapsed pit (infested blocks under a walkway).
- **Quiet rooms** between fights: a sanctuary with a normal vault opened by the keys won elsewhere, a dead adventurer's camp (lore book, a clue to a secret room).
- **Boss branch**: the jigsaw does not know where the dungeon ends. A dedicated chain from `room_1` (sealed descent, corridor, antechamber, arena) guarantees one boss per dungeon. A vault door in the antechamber that needs the keys of other rooms makes the player explore first (see step 3).

### Step 2: Combat with trial spawners and dungeon loot tables
- **Themed trial spawners per room**: magma cubes and blazes in the forge, vexes and evokers in the library, wither skeletons in the maze (done: slimes in the well, skeletons and wither skeletons in the crypt). Trial spawners spawn waves scaled to the number of players, go on cooldown once beaten and eject a reward.
- **Equipped mobs**: mobs spawned by a trial spawner can wear gear from a loot table (enchanted bows for skeletons, armored zombies...).
- **Elites**: the `scale` attribute (since 1.20.5) allows bigger mini-bosses, such as a giant wither skeleton with more health and a custom name.
- **Ominous mode**: with Bad Omen, the dungeon gets harder and gives better rewards, like Trial Chambers.
- **Dedicated `dungeon:` loot tables** for every room type (forge, library, maze, secret rooms) instead of village and stronghold tables.

> Before writing trial spawner configurations, check their exact format in 26.3: it has changed since 1.21.

### Step 3: Rewards and the final room
- **Dungeon key**: vaults accept any item as a key, for example a custom "Dungeon Key" with its own name and model. Room trial spawners drop it when beaten.
- **Vaults** with their own loot tables, including ominous vaults. Ominous vaults go in some secret rooms (and other special places): the room vaults (well, crypt) stay normal, and ominous spawners eject normal keys for them. To reward ominous mode, the ominous configs will also need to eject ominous keys for those vaults.
- **Exclusive enchantment**: enchantments are data-driven since 1.21. A custom enchantment only found in this dungeon gives a reason to explore it.
- **Final room**: a boss arena with ominous vaults, reached after beating rooms to collect keys. Best built in game, then integrated (jigsaws, pools, weights).

### Step 4: Variety
- **Dungeon themes** with jigsaw `pool_aliases` (since 1.20.5): a whole dungeon picks one theme (undead, illagers, Nether), and mobs and loot follow it. Aliases can also give each dungeon its own subset of rooms (kitchen or forge, library or crypt) so the same rooms come back less often. Wait until there are more rooms: with 7, a subset makes each dungeon poorer.
- **Processors** that randomly add moss and cobwebs and swap some full blocks for slabs (worn look), so each dungeon looks different with the same rooms. Never cracked bricks: they mark secret rooms.
- **Advancements**: find the dungeon, beat the boss, find every secret room.

## Open points
- `corridor/cross_1` is not used by any pool and its south exit leads nowhere. It looks like an old draft of `small_cross_1`: delete it or add it to a pool.
- The structure files were saved in 1.20.1 / 1.20.2 and are upgraded by the game when loaded. Re-saving them with a structure block in 26.3 would remove that dependency.
